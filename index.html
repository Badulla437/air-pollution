About the Project

Air pollution is one of the biggest environmental and public-health challenges today. Our CSP project studies the problem in our community and spreads awareness about it.

🎯

Objective

Identify the main sources of air pollution around us and help people understand its effects.

🔍

What we did

Observed the area, collected information, spoke to residents and analysed the findings.

📣

Outcome

Community awareness and simple, practical steps to reduce pollution.

Air Quality Index scale
Good → Satisfactory → Moderate → Poor → Very Poor → Severe
😷

PM2.5

Fine particles enter the lungs and bloodstream and are among the most harmful pollutants.

🚗

Vehicles

Traffic exhaust is a major source of NO₂, CO and particulate matter.

🔥

Burning

Open burning of waste and crop residue adds smoke and toxic gases.

🏭

Industry

Factories and power plants release SO₂ and other pollutants if uncontrolled.

🫁

Health

Linked to asthma, heart disease and reduced lung function, especially in children.

🌳

Solutions

Trees, public transport, clean fuel and no open burning make a real difference.

Project Progress – 8 Weeks

Our week-by-week journey.

Week 1 – Topic selection & team formation

Finalised "Air Pollution" and divided roles among the 5 members.

Week 2 – Literature study

Read about pollutants, their sources and the air quality index.

Week 3 – Survey planning

Chose locations to observe and prepared a questionnaire.

Week 4 – Data collection

Visited selected places and recorded observations and responses.

Week 5 – Data analysis

Organised the data and identified the main pollution sources.

Week 6 – Awareness activity

Conducted an awareness drive with posters and talks.

Week 7 – Report preparation

Wrote the project report and prepared the presentation.

Week 8 – Final presentation & website

Presented the project and published this website.

Conclusion

Through this CSP project we learned that air pollution affects everyone, and that small actions by individuals and communities add up to real change.

The project also taught our team teamwork, field research and social responsibility.
